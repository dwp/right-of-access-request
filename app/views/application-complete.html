What happens next

We’ll check the identity information you’ve given us and find the information you’ve asked us for.

When you’ll receive your response

We’ll aim to send your information within 1 month. Complex requests can take longer to complete. If we need more time, we’ll let you know within the first month.

How we’ll send your information

If you asked for your information online, we’ll send you a link by email to download files from a secure web page.

If you asked for information in an alternative format, we may contact you to find out more about what you need.

What you’ll get

We’ll send you a letter as part of your information pack that tells you:

  • what’s included (if we can’t give you something you’ve asked for, we’ll explain why)
  • how to contact us if you think something’s missing
  • how to complain
